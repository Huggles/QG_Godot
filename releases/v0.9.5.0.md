**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.5.0** (beta)

**New**
- Skirmish has an AI difficulty picker: Normal or Hard (Hard is the default)
- A strip under the faction bar shows the turn's steps; hover a step to read its rule, and see which factions are being asked to decide
- New setting "Only Ask When I Can React" (Settings > Gameplay), on by default: you are only prompted when you hold a card that can react

- Behind the scenes: the groundwork for expansions and add-ons is in place, ready for future content

**Changed**
- Choosing a faction now shows flag cards in your hand, in turn order, instead of a pop-up
- Cards in the draw and discard windows are shown smaller, so more fit on screen

**Fixed**
- A faction no longer scores in its Victory step while an enemy Army holds its Home space
- Cards that protect a piece for the turn (Moscow, Stalingrad, Leningrad, Monte Cassino, Radar, RAF and others) now work for every player in multiplayer and after a rejoin; protected pieces show a shield
- The faction strip no longer grows a little every time it refreshes
