**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.8.4** (beta)

**New**
- Faction flags show each faction's deck, status and response counts on labelled pills; the hand, score and reserve pieces are in the slide-out
- Hover a flag's deck, status or response pill to see those cards in a popup; face-down responses show as card backs
- A Menu button in the bottom-left bar opens the game menu
- New main menu background art

**Changed**
- The history close-up card is a little smaller, so it covers less of the board while staying readable
- The resolution picker shows your screen's resolution while fullscreen is selected

**Fixed**
- Accepting a Steam invite while the game is closed now takes you straight into the lobby
- Long card titles no longer overlap the card art
