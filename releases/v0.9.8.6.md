**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.8.6** (beta)

**New**
- Faction Banner Size setting scales the faction flags from 80% to 120%

**Changed**
- Each faction flag now shows its played status and response cards as two small fans on top; hover a fan to see the cards
- The deck counter on each flag shows a card stack icon, and the flags sit further apart to make room

**Fixed**
- The game no longer freezes for a moment while a Hard AI decides its move on a large board
