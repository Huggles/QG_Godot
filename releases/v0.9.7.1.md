**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.7.1** (beta)

**Changed**
- Slow presentation speed is snappier: unit burns, camera moves and card pauses take 2s instead of 3s, and short events 1s instead of 2s
- Unit deploys take the same time as other medium-length events, so on Fast they take 0.5s instead of 0.2s
- The turn badge fades in and out more quickly, and played cards stay on screen for a medium pause before moving to their slot
- The "Waiting for" list, the chat box, played cards and the loading screen now fade and slide at a steady half second, whatever presentation speed you pick

**Fixed**
- Radar now triggers only when your supplied US Navy is battled, not when it is removed by Kamikaze or by losing supply
- Only the player who played Reallocate Resources sees the card list it opens
