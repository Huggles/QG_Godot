**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.7.5** (beta)

**New**
- Factions now fly as pennants from a flagpole down the left edge, with the score on each flag. The tail grows with every point, so you can see the score race at a glance
- Each pennant shows that faction's cards in hand, deck and on the table plus reserve pieces, with extra counters for Air Forces and minor factions
- Click a faction's flag to open a Score History window: every faction's running total by round on one chart, with a per-round breakdown
- The players panel shows the flags of the factions each player controls, lined up in columns next to their name and ping

**Changed**
- New look for the main menu, skirmish, lobby and all other menu screens and dialogs: brass on dark olive, frosted panels and animated brass trim
- The Settings dialog is laid out as tidy labelled rows and no longer resizes when you switch tabs
- On the skirmish screen the Axis seats now default to AI
- When choosing a battle target, empty countries glow faintly and pulse less often, so the markers on attackable units stand out. Hovering an empty country lights it orange, and a real target lights green
- Hovering a build, recruit or deploy card now gives its countries a faint glow instead of the full pulse

**Fixed**
- Faction flags on the skirmish seats were not showing
