**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.7.6** (beta)

**New**
- Score History now has its own button on the top-left toolbar, next to the deck button
- New Gameplay setting, Open Faction Banners, to have the faction pennants start slid open instead of tucked

**Changed**
- Faction pennants now rest tucked behind their flag. Click anywhere on a pennant to slide its tail out with the card and reserve counts, and click again to tuck it away
- Pennant counts are white with a black outline on every faction, so they read clearly on all colours
- The pennants are plain cloth now, without the brass trim. The faction on turn still flies harder than the rest

**Fixed**
- The hand card preview no longer hides behind the faction pennants
