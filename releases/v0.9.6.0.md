**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.6.0** (beta)

**🎖️ Total War expansion: early beta**
The first playable version of the Total War expansion is here for everyone with a Total War key. Once it's redeemed on Steam, the expansion downloads alongside the game and its banner appears on the main menu.
- Tick Total War when hosting a game or starting a skirmish
- France and China join the war
- Air Forces, plus a new Air step each turn: deploy, marshal and fight for Air Superiority
- New Air Power and Bolster cards: 44 of the expansion's 165 cards so far, including BETASOM, P-51 Mustang, Pact of Steel and Golden Square Coup
- Cards used "at the beginning of your Air, Victory or Draw step" get their chance at the start of that step

This is a work in progress: the remaining cards follow in later updates, and some art and Air Power card counts are placeholders. Everyone in a multiplayer game needs the expansion to join a Total War game. Please report anything odd!

**Changed**
- Multiplayer games always use the standard scenario; the lobby's scenario picker is gone
- The in-game Debug menu is no longer shown
