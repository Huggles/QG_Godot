**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.4.3** (beta)

**Changed**
- The turn badge shows for less time: 3, 2 or 1 seconds at Slow, Normal or Fast speed
- The forced discard window sorts cards in the same order as your hand
- Bots now value Status cards that score VP each turn (Impero Italiano, Mare Nostrum, ...), so they play and feed them more

**Fixed**
- The UK card Dutch East Indies was missing from the game and is now in the UK deck: it recruits an army in Indonesia, an army in New Guinea, then a navy in the South China Sea
  - Free French Allies, which had taken its place, is now in the US deck and still recruits a United Kingdom army
- Pop-up windows now take clicks over everything they cover, and the chat no longer sits on top of them
