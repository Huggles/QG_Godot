**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.2.4** (beta)

**New**
- Victory points now animate onto the board as they are scored, for the Victory step and for every scoring card
- Points scored by a unit now show on that unit, and points with no specific source show on the faction's home space
- Deploy unit animation changed
- Remove unit animation changed
- The game history now shows which cards were drawn

**Changed**
- New players start in fullscreen (existing settings are kept)
- Active cards are now shown as a fan by default (existing settings are kept)
- Pop-up windows and unit deploy/remove animations are faster
- Cards in the history line are smaller

**Fixed**
- Blitzkrieg now triggers only on land battles, not sea battles
- Several fixes for quitting, restarting and rejoining games
- In online games, scoring animations now play at the same time for everyone
- Rebuilding a unit where it already stands no longer leaves it wearing a leftover bullseye
