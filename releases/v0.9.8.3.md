**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.8.3** (beta)

**Changed**
- Played Status and Response cards now zoom into the history strip like other cards, instead of staying in the middle of the screen
- The card in the middle of the screen fades while you hover it, and clicks pass through to the board underneath
- Rosie the Riveter's cards are now picked straight from your hand fan
- Docked pop-ups line up in one row under the history strip and no longer cover its badges
- The history close-up card is bigger, so its text is readable at 1080p
- The amber caution glow stands out more
- Volume sliders now scale with how loud things actually sound; 50% really is about half as loud
- Country labels and the players panel are on by default the first time you launch the game
- AI seats no longer skip building on supply stars a teammate could also reach

**Fixed**
- Japan's numbers on history badges are readable again
- Card titles and text no longer run into the card edges
- A thin grey line no longer shows at the right edge of card art
- The close X sits centred on its button
