**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.7.2** (beta)

**New**
- Ranked games: a Steam lobby game with at least two players, no bots and no expansions now changes your leaderboard rating when it ends
- The winning team gains 10 rating and the losing team loses 10
- Earn +1 each for: top VP scorer on your team, best single turn of the game, most enemy units eliminated on your team, and most enemy discards caused on your team (at least 3). Ties share the bonus
- The victory screen shows how much your rating changed
- Single player, the tutorial and direct-IP games are never ranked, and a loaded save keeps the ranked status it started with

**Fixed**
- A failed leaderboard download no longer resets a rating below 1500 back up to 1500
