**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.8.0** (beta)

**New**
- Tutorial button on the main menu, with its own screen listing every tutorial and what it covers
- Each faction pennant now shows the name of the player (or AI) playing it
- Cards drawn this round or last get a folded corner in your hand
- The history popup draws an arrow from the played card to its targets
- Total War: the discard confirm button now says how many VP discarding nothing costs
- The game version is shown at the bottom left of the main menu
- New loading screen art

**Changed**
- The in-game HUD and pop-ups have a new look in the main menu's style
- New HUD layout: team scores top left, players panel at the top, menu and chat bottom left
- Draw and discard pop-ups that time out now shrink into the history strip instead of just fading
- The round tracker is a small pill just under the turn steps
- Tutorial texts rewritten to match the current game, and the arrows now point at the right parts of the new HUD
- "Game turn advanced to" entries no longer clutter the game history
- Skirmish no longer lists the tutorials

**Fixed**
- Pop-ups no longer leave a blurred block or halo on screen as they close
