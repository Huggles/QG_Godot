**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.7.0** (beta)

**New**
- A deck button on the in-game menu opens the rulebook's card distribution table: how many cards of each type every faction started with
- Total War: each faction row shows how many Air Forces it still has in reserve, France and China included
- The round is now shown as a track under the team totals ("Round 7 of 20"), turning amber on the last round
- Reallocate Resources shows how many of each card are left
- Pop-up Card Size setting on the Gameplay tab (70% to 120%) for draw, discard and card-choice pop-ups

**Changed**
- Played and activated cards now slide into the card slot at the top instead of opening a pop-up; Status and Response cards still on the table then shrink into their faction's Active Cards button
- Pop-up cards default to 100% size, and pop-ups with many cards scroll instead of growing past the screen; cards are spread evenly across rows
- The reaction-skip "A" toggle is gone from the faction rows; choose the skip on the prompt itself, or use "Only Ask When I Can React" in the settings
- Total War decks now match the rulebook's card counts for Air Power, Build Army, Build Navy and Land Battle

**Fixed**
- The input chime no longer plays for prompts that are skipped automatically
- The "Waiting on" text no longer piles up factions that already answered
- Scrolling the mouse wheel over a pop-up no longer zooms the board
- The Gameplay settings tab scrolls, so Close is no longer pushed off a 1080p screen
- Quitting to the menu while an AI player was thinking no longer shows an error
