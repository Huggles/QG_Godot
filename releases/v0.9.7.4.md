**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.7.4** (beta)

**New**
26 new Steam achievements. They don't unlock in the tutorial, or when one player controls factions on both teams.

*Easy*
- First Victory: win a game
- German, British, Japanese, Soviet, Italian and American Victory: win a game as that faction
- First Blood: make the first elimination of a game
- D-Day: have a UK or US Army in Western Europe
- Strategic Bombing: as the US, force one enemy faction to discard 5 cards in a round
- Blockade: as the UK, force one enemy faction to discard 4 cards in a round
- Cut Off: have an enemy faction lose a unit to lack of supply

*Medium*
- Decisive Victory: win by a 30-point lead
- Operation Barbarossa: have a German Army in Moscow
- War of Attrition: eliminate 100 enemy units across all your games
- Battle of the Atlantic: as Germany, force one enemy faction to discard 5 cards in a round
- Mare Nostrum: as Italy, force one enemy faction to discard 3 cards in a round
- Economic Stranglehold: with one faction, force enemies to discard 10 cards in a game
- Encirclement: have an enemy faction lose 2 units to lack of supply in one turn

*Hard*
- Lightning War: win by a 30-point lead by the end of round 8
- Against All Odds: win after trailing by 20+ points at the end of a round
- Downfall: have a US Army in Japan
- Veteran Commander: win 10 games
- Doolittle Raid: as the US, make the first elimination of a game
- Indian Ocean Raid: as Japan, force one enemy faction to discard 5 cards in a round
- The Pocket: have an enemy faction lose 3 units to lack of supply in one turn
