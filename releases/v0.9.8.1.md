**Quartermaster General WW2 — v0.9.8.1** (beta)

**New**
- Tutorial 2: Status and Response cards, found in the new Tutorial menu
- Games open with a short intro that flies to each capital and names its faction and player
- The multiplayer lobby has a chat panel
- The host's Start runs a 5-second countdown in lobby chat, and faction picks lock while it runs
- Land and sea battles have new explosion, splash and sinking effects
- Bletchley Park and Keep Calm and Carry On are now in the UK's Total War deck

**Changed**
- You now pick discards straight from your hand fan instead of a pop-up
- Cards in hand slide, fade in and fade out as your hand changes
- Battle cards with only empty spaces to attack get a caution glow; unplayable cards get a red one
- AI seats are listed as "AI - Hard" / "AI - Normal" everywhere
- AI seats pause a moment before playing, so their turns are easier to follow
- AI seats play smarter: they value Status and Response cards, discard their weakest cards, avoid running out of cards and weigh threatened supply stars
- Tutorial 1 and 2 messages are shorter, so the panel no longer covers your hand

**Fixed**
- A card that gets cancelled (ASW Tactics, Bletchley Park, Keep Calm and Carry On) no longer charges its discard or other price
- Long games against the AI no longer slow down and eat up memory
- The discard confirm button no longer hangs over your cards
